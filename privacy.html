Arrow Escape: Puzzle Out – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026. Developer: Mudit Kumar. Contact: techshivam0616@gmail.com

What the game stores

Your level progress, hints and settings are saved only on your device. We run no servers, require no account, and do not collect your name, email, contacts or location.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). Unity may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic device and usage information to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure performance and prevent fraud. You can choose Allow or Limit for personalised ads in the app under Settings → Privacy & ad choices; your choice is passed to Unity Ads as its GDPR and CCPA consent signal. See Unity's privacy policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can change the Allow or Limit choice for personalised ads at any time in the app (Settings → Privacy & ad choices). You can also reset your advertising ID in your Android settings. Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored by the game.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
