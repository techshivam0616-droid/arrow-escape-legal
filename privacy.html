Arrow Escape: Puzzle Out – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026. Developer: Mudit Kumar. Contact: techshivam0616@gmail.com

What the game stores

Your level progress, hints and settings are saved only on your device. We run no servers, require no account, and do not collect your name, email, contacts or location.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by third-party ad networks, including Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) and Google AdMob. These may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic device and usage information to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure performance and prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. See Unity's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored by the game.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
